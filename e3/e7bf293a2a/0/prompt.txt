From Apple: the camera never rushes. Features are held long 
enough to be understood. Dark space is a design element.

From Figma: motion shows relationships. The transcript and 
claim field filling simultaneously makes the connection visible.

From Canva: the human moment (car, waveform, person) is not 
rushed. The software exists to serve that person. Earn the 
right to show the software by establishing the human first.

From Legora: enterprise software can feel premium. Glass, 
grain, and considered motion make the product feel trustworthy 
and inevitable — not a prototype, not a hackathon project.
